Common questions

Quick answers about tracking, budgets, and reports

How does SpendSense organize expenses?

Expenses are grouped by category and by date, so budgets and reports reflect exactly what you logged.

Can I add my own categories?

Yes. Open Categories, give it a name, choose expense or income, and save.

How do budget alerts work?

When a category reaches 80% of its monthly limit — or goes over — it appears in Budget alerts on the Reports page.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Open Settings and pick a currency; amounts across the app update to that symbol.

Where can I review spending trends?

Open Reports to see spending by category, income vs expenses, and budget progress for the month.

Does dark mode work across pages?

Yes. Use the toggle in the top bar or Settings, and your choice is remembered on this device.
