Common questions

Quick answers about tracking, budgets, reports, and account access

How does SpendSense organize expenses?

Expenses are grouped by the category you choose and by their dates, so budgets and reports reflect exactly what you logged.

Can I add my own categories?

Yes. Open Categories, give the category a name, choose expense or income, and save. You can also create a category while adding an expense.

How do budget alerts work?

When a category reaches 80% of its monthly limit — or goes over — it appears in Budget alerts on the Reports page. SpendSense does not send email or push alerts.

Can I change the currency?

You can change the display currency in Settings. This changes the currency symbol shown across the app; it does not convert or rewrite the amounts already stored.

Where can I review spending trends?

Open Reports to see spending by category, income versus expenses, and budget progress for the current month compared with the previous month.

Does dark mode work across pages?

Yes. Use the theme control in any top bar or in Settings. Your palette and light/dark choice are remembered on this device.

Can I reset my password in the app?

No. Self-service password reset is not implemented yet, so SpendSense cannot send reset emails. The password-help page explains the available options.

Does SpendSense send notifications?

Not yet. There is no in-app notification feed or reminder scheduler. Budget alerts are available on the Reports page instead.
