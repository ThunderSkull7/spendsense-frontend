How linkage and counting work

One schedule, at most one tracker

  • A linked tracker derives amount, frequency, category and next renewal from exactly one recurring expense.
  • A recurring expense can only be linked once, so a single obligation is not double-counted in tracker totals.
  • Paused or cancelled trackers, and trackers whose bill is paused or stopped, are excluded from cost estimates.
  • Changing a tracker status never changes the underlying bill, pays a bill or creates an expense.
  • Renewal amounts are expectations. Actual paid amounts remain on the Bills page and in expense history.

Provider cancellation

SpendSense cannot cancel a subscription with a provider. You must cancel directly with the provider, then record the change here for your own tracking.

Cancelling a tracker preserves paid history and does not remove any expense. There is no automatic expense creation, bank connection or provider integration.
