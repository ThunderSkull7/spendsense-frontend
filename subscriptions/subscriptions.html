Linkage and Expense Isolation

  • Linked Subscriptions: Derive amount, billing frequency, category, and renewal dates directly from their recurring expense schedule. Each bill can be linked to at most one subscription tracker.
  • Standalone Trackers: Maintain their own renewal date and amount. They are tracking entries only and never write to the expense ledger.
  • No Duplicate Expenses: Modifying, pausing, or cancelling a subscription tracker never creates, alters, or pays an expense transaction.

External Provider Notice

SpendSense is a tracking tool, not a payment gateway.

Cancelling or pausing a subscription here updates your financial tracking only. You must cancel or modify memberships directly with the service provider (e.g., Netflix, Spotify).
